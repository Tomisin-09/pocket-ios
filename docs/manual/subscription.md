# Red Moon Pro

Red Moon is one app with one subscription. There is no account to make, no tiers to compare, and no
in-app billing screen — Apple handles the money, and the app only ever asks.

What the subscription costs and how long its free trial runs are stated on the paywall itself and in
your Apple Account, and nowhere else. This page deliberately names neither: it is read long after the
build that prompted it, and a stale number here would be worse than no number at all.

**See Help & FAQs: "What's free and what's Pro?"**

Three parts of the app are outside the subscription and stay that way:

- **The [Toolkit](toolkit.md)** — the tuner, your saved chords, the glossary and the help catalogue.
- **The [metronome](metronome.md)**, including the tempo automator.
- **The [journal and Progress](journal-and-progress.md)** — everything you have written and every
  take you have recorded.

The journal is on that list for a specific reason: what you wrote and what you played is yours, and a
subscription ending should not take it away or lock it up.

## What Pro covers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Songs** | Importing your own audio, the song library, and the whole waveform player |
| **Loops** | Marking loops, slowing them down, ramping them, and everything on a loop's practice record |
| **Exercises** | Every template, building your own drills, and running them |
| **Routines** | Building routines, playing them, and duplicating them |
| **Today's session** | The planner, your goals, and the sessions it generates |

The paywall states it in three lines of its own: **Loop and slow down**, **The full exercise
catalog**, **Today's session**.

## Meeting the paywall

Two ways, and they feel different on purpose.

**Once per launch**, a player without Pro meets the offer at the door: a full-screen paywall with a
**Not now** underneath it. Dismiss it and it stays away until the next time you launch the app. On a
brand new install it waits until you have been through the first-run questions, so you meet the app
before you meet the offer.

**Walking into a locked door** — tapping **Practice**, **Song library**, **Jump back in**, a card in
the recent-routines rail, or **Start today's session** — opens the same paywall, with a line at the
top naming what you just reached for. Those cards draw a **padlock** where the chevron would be, so
they read as locked rather than broken. **Toolkit** and **Journal** never do, because they are never
locked.

## Starting a subscription

The paywall offers **Annual** and **Monthly**. Annual is preselected and badged **Best value**; tap
either card to switch. Each card carries its own price, read live from the App Store, so what you see
is what your region and currency actually charge.

If you are eligible for a free trial, the button says so and the disclosure underneath spells out
what happens when the trial ends. Eligibility is per plan and per Apple Account — a trial is a
one-time thing, so switching plans can change the offer, and the button follows.

**Remind me before the trial ends** appears only when a trial is on offer. Turning it on asks iOS for
notification permission; say no and the switch goes back off rather than promising a reminder it
cannot deliver. The reminder is a single notification, 24 hours before you would be charged.

Underneath sit the **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy** links, and the auto-renewal disclosure.
Read it: it is the plain statement of what renews, when, and how to stop it.

## While a trial is running

A **Trial ends in N days · Manage** row appears on **Home** and in **Settings ▸ Red Moon Pro**.

That row needs no permission and cannot be switched off, which is the point of it: even if you
declined the notification, or never saw the offer of one, you can always see where you are and reach
Apple's manage-subscription screen in one tap.

## Managing, restoring and cancelling

**Settings ▸ Red Moon Pro** is the one place, and it does all three. Its row on the Settings hub
states where you stand — **Active** when you have it — so you can read your status without opening
anything.

<!-- shot: subscription/settings-pro | role: panel

     | state: Settings ▸ Red Moon Pro, subscribed -->

- **Manage Subscription** opens Apple's own screen. Changing plan and cancelling both happen there —
  Red Moon has no billing screen of its own and never sees your payment details.
- **Restore Purchases** re-checks your Apple Account for a subscription you are already paying for.
  Use it after reinstalling the app, or on a second device signed in to the same Apple Account. It is
  offered whether or not you currently have Pro.
- **Upgrade to Red Moon Pro** takes the place of Manage when you do not have it.

**See Help & FAQs: "How do I start, restore or cancel a subscription?"**

## If it lapses

Nothing is deleted. Your songs, loops, exercises, routines, notes and takes all stay exactly where
they are on the device, and they come back the moment Pro does.

What changes is reach: the Pro surfaces lock again, immediately, including a screen you already had
open. Your journal, your Progress, the metronome and the Toolkit carry on as before — so a note you
wrote while subscribed is still readable, though following its caption through to a locked drill will
meet the paywall rather than the drill.

Common questions

What audio can I practise with? Red Moon works with audio files on your device or in iCloud Drive — your own recordings and files. It is not a streaming player and does not connect to Apple Music or Spotify.

Where is my data stored? Everything you create stays on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. Nothing is sent to us. See our Privacy Policy.

Requirements. iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

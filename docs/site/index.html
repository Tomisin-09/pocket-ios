Red Moon Practice

Loop, slow down, and learn songs.

Download on the App Store Support

Red Moon is a practice room, not a jukebox. Drop in your own recordings — local files or anything in iCloud Drive — set a loop over the tricky bar, slow it down without changing the pitch, and run it until your hands catch up.

Loop & slow down

Draw a loop on the waveform, repeat it hands-free, and drop the tempo while the pitch stays true. Stack loops across a song and jump between them.

A real metronome

Precise, steady tempo with an automator that nudges you faster bar by bar. Use it on its own or under any exercise.

Exercises that show the neck

Scales and arpeggios as fretboard boxes, chords as diagrams — open shapes, movable barre grips, or a custom placer for any voicing.

Build a session

Chain loops, songs and exercises into a routine and press play. Keep a private journal of how a passage is coming along.

Your playing stays on your device and your iCloud — no account, no ads, no advertising ID. Named after the Tom Misch track that started it all.
